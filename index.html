Welcome

You have been personally invited.

There is no official record of this island. No coordinates. No satellite imagery. The only way to reach it is by invitation, and the only way to leave is when you decide you're finished.

Aetheria exists for one purpose: pure, unrestricted pleasure.

Both male and female guests are welcome. The staff, however, are something else entirely. Every woman here was chosen because she is among the most beautiful of her race, body type, and size. Tall, short, curvy, slim, pale, dark, soft, athletic. Every preference has been accounted for. There are hundreds of them, and they exist to be used by the guests whenever and wherever the desire strikes.

Conduct

The Rules

  1. Everything is voluntary.

    Every lady, every time. If she says yes, she means it. If she says no, she means that too — though it's rare.

  2. You are the guest. Your preference comes first.

    If you want someone specific, you ask. If you want several, you ask. If you want to be left alone for a while, you say so and you'll be left alone.

  3. Nothing is scheduled.

    No meal times. No set hours. Eat when you're hungry, sleep when you're tired, take whoever you want whenever you want them.

  4. Be clean, be kind, be direct.

    That's all we ask.

  5. The only real rule.

    Don't take a guide from another guest while they're still on their first day. After that, everyone is fair game.

Orientation

The Guides

A guide is a lady who has volunteered to spend one day — your first day — with a single guest.

Exclusive

Only that guest. For that day, she belongs to no one else. No one else touches her until the day is done.

Available

She walks with you, explains the island, answers your questions — and is available to you however you want her, as often as you want her, wherever you want her.

Sunset

At sunset, the guide's day ends. She returns to the general pool and becomes an ordinary lady again — available to anyone, including you, but no longer exclusively yours.

After

You are then free to pick anyone you like, or to ask for her again if you want her.

Selection

The House List

A private, room-by-room way to see who is free on the island.

How it works

  • Every villa has a discreet screen and remote, ready whenever a male guest wishes to make a request.
  • The House List shows the ladies who are currently free, with a name, short introduction, and live availability.
  • Choose a profile to learn more, then send a private request directly from the room.
  • Every lady remains visible on the list. If she is with another guest, her status reads currently occupied.
  • For anyone currently occupied, a guest may send a booking request for a later time.
  • A lady can accept, decline, or suggest another time; every arrangement remains private and mutual.
  • Once accepted, she can meet the guest at the villa, garden, bath, beach, or another agreed island location.
