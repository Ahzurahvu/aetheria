The Scenario Archive / 2096

Storylines & Prompts

Choose an ongoing campaign or a single encounter in Kuroshiro. Each premise supports an adult female lead and a male partner with flexible roles, original characters, and collaborative storytelling. Stories emphasize character agency: official assignments never imply personal or intimate consent.

01 / Multi-session arcs

Long-Term Storylines

Character relationships, changing allegiances, mysteries, and major decisions that unfold across multiple scenes.

LONG / 01

The Altered Assignment

Political intrigue · Slow-burn romance

An adult participant arrives to discover her residence and E.V.E. file were changed by an unknown Directorate official. A male administrator investigating the alteration becomes both her best lead and someone she cannot fully trust.

Opening hook

A black government car waits at the residential entrance. Its passenger knows her name before she has introduced herself.

Long-term arc: Who changed the file—and who benefits from keeping it changed?

LONG / 02

The Glass Apartment

Social drama · Privilege

A woman unexpectedly receives High Status accommodation despite a modest R-classification. A powerful patron claims to have helped, while neighbors suspect the apartment is part of a bargain she never made.

Opening hook

A welcome basket arrives in the luxury residence. Inside is a key that opens a room not listed on her floor plan.

Long-term arc: Can privilege ever be accepted without surrendering independence?

LONG / 03

The E.V.E. Exception

Mystery · Conspiracy

An E.V.E. analyst and a participant discover an impossible compatibility result. Their investigation exposes manipulated assessments throughout the Black Zone.

Opening hook

The algorithm flags their case as 'EXCEPTION: HUMAN REVIEW PROHIBITED.'

Long-term arc: Will they reveal the system's flaw or use it to protect someone?

LONG / 04

Beyond the Checkpoint

Escape · Trust · Romance

A resident from Sector C meets a male transit inspector who knows of a gap in the nightly checkpoint logs. What begins as a request for a small favor becomes a dangerous alliance.

Opening hook

At 11:59 p.m., a gate opens for exactly sixty seconds.

Long-term arc: What does freedom cost, and whom can they trust?

LONG / 05

The Directorate's Heir

Enemies to allies · Court politics

The son of an influential family is appointed to oversee a troubled residential sector. A female resident organizes a peaceful challenge to unfair assignments, forcing them into repeated negotiations.

Opening hook

Their first meeting begins with a petition signed by half the building.

Long-term arc: Can two people on opposite sides change the rules without becoming tools of them?

LONG / 06

The Missing Resident

Thriller · Investigation

When a woman disappears from the registry but her apartment remains occupied, a male investigator and her closest female friend follow a trail of altered security footage.

Opening hook

The apartment lights turn on every night, although her record says she left weeks ago.

Long-term arc: Is she hiding, detained, or deliberately erased?

03 / Mature dystopian campaigns

Dark Story Arcs

Five mature, non-graphic premises exploring exploitation of authority, coercion, corruption, romance, and resistance. These stories treat abuse as a conflict with consequences, not as implied consent or an intimate privilege of rank.

DARK / 01

The Nightly Visitor

Corruption · Blackmail · Survival

A Directorate official repeatedly exploits a Low Status resident's vulnerable position, threatening her housing and livelihood if she reports him. She begins quietly documenting his visits while a new contact inside the administration offers a chance to challenge his authority.

Opening hook

At 11:47 p.m., the familiar security override clicks. Tonight, however, the resident has hidden a recording device.

Long-term arc: Will the evidence protect her—or expose a larger cover-up?

DARK / 02

An Apex Under Contract

Elite influence · Reproductive control · Resistance

An influential businessman has manipulated an Apex participant's medical and administrative records to keep her assigned under his patronage, including coercive reproductive decisions. She discovers an irregularity in the paperwork and searches for an ally who can help restore her autonomy.

Opening hook

The latest reassignment notice arrives with her signature already filled in. She never signed it.

Long-term arc: Who authorized the forgery, and how can she break the arrangement?

DARK / 03

The Repeat Applicant

Pattern of exploitation · Investigation

A well-connected man repeatedly requests new participants and then abandons their cases once his influence has exhausted their privileges. When multiple women compare their experiences, an unsettling pattern emerges.

Opening hook

A Directorate clerk notices the same name on six unrelated reassignment files.

Long-term arc: Can the women and a sympathetic investigator establish the pattern before more records disappear?

DARK / 04

A Stolen Reunion

Forbidden romance · Security conspiracy

A security officer discovers that his adult girlfriend was selected for Project Rebirth. He secretly alters her access status so they can meet, putting his career and her safety at risk. Their attempts to undo the enrolment draw the attention of E.V.E.

Opening hook

A familiar voice speaks over the apartment intercom: 'It's me. Don't open the door until the corridor camera turns away.'

Long-term arc: Will they expose the system or find a lawful way out together?

DARK / 05

The Lowest Classification

Resistance · Retaliation · Solidarity

After organizing resistance to the programme, a woman is punished with a downgrade to the most restrictive assignment class. Officials treat the downgrade as a way to strip her of protection. She forms an alliance with other participants to document abuses and seek an appeal.

Opening hook

The downgrade order is pinned to her door. Beneath it, someone has written: 'You're not alone.'

Long-term arc: Can she reclaim control of her life and challenge the system that targeted her?

02 / One scene to a few sessions

Short-Term Prompts

Focused scenarios designed to launch a conversation quickly. Each can stand alone or develop into a longer campaign.

SHORT / 01

The Wrong Door

Meet-cute · Mystery

A housing key leads to the wrong apartment—and a stranger holding another copy.

Opening hook

The lock clicks open. Someone inside says, 'I was told you wouldn't arrive until tomorrow.'

SHORT / 02

Midnight Inspection

Tension · Secrets

A surprise facilities inspection catches two characters inside a restricted corridor.

Opening hook

Footsteps approach; a red security light switches on.

SHORT / 03

Rooftop in the Rain

Slow-burn · Conversation

Two adults find shelter on the same rooftop terrace during a sudden downpour.

Opening hook

'You come here to escape the cameras too?'

SHORT / 04

The Housing Appeal

Drama · Negotiation

A participant challenges a residential reassignment before an official who may have his own agenda.

Opening hook

The appeal form already bears a signature she doesn't recognize.

SHORT / 05

One Night at the Gala

Formal event · Intrigue

An invitation to a Directorate charity gala places two strangers at the same table.

Opening hook

A waiter delivers an envelope addressed to both of them.

SHORT / 06

The Lift Stops

Character study · Suspense

A power interruption traps two people in a residential elevator.

Opening hook

The emergency speaker connects—but the voice on the other end knows their names.

SHORT / 07

An Unsent Letter

Emotion · Reunion

A misdelivered letter reveals a secret from before Project Rebirth.

Opening hook

'I wasn't supposed to read it. But I think it was meant for you.'

SHORT / 08

Curfew Exception

Slice of life · Adventure

A clerical error grants a resident temporary permission to visit Kuroshiro's public waterfront.

Opening hook

Her pass expires in ninety minutes, and she has one place she desperately wants to see.

Using a prompt

Choose your adult characters and their roles, agree on boundaries and tone, and decide whether the scene takes place in the Black Zone or elsewhere in Kuroshiro. You can use an existing registry character or create a new one. The R-classification and housing status are separate systems.
