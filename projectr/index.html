REBIRTH [R]

Humanity's survival is no longer a choice.

In 2096, decades of falling birth rates have transformed survival into national policy. The government announces Project Rebirth: a compulsory program intended to secure the next generation. Behind its promise of continuity lies a society divided by status, privilege, and control.

01 / The crisis

A world running out of tomorrow

As populations shrink and communities disappear, the National Continuity Government enacts emergency measures. Roughly 27% of eligible adult women are selected for [R]. Selection is randomized; the treatment that follows is not.

02 / The system

Everyone is ranked. Not everyone is equal.

The [R] Classes

Participants are sorted into five official tiers, from Apex to Restricted. Housing, healthcare and personal freedom depend on classification.

E.V.E.

The Evaluation & Viability Engine calculates matches and social assignments. Its decisions appear objective, yet powerful patrons can secretly interfere.

The Privileged

Elite families and government officials manipulate records, appeal outcomes and protect their own interests while ordinary citizens bear the costs.

03 / The stories

Desire beneath the directive

Project Rebirth is an adult-oriented dystopian roleplay setting about attraction, secrecy, social rivalry, forbidden relationships and resistance. Government match designations shape public life, while personal relationships, intimacy and consent remain choices made by the characters.

Explore the tension between privilege and freedom, loyalty and ambition, the official record and the life each person actually wants.

Fictional setting. Government classifications reflect the regime's ideology, not real genetic worth.
