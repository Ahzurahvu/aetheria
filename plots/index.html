ONE-SHOT 01

Hinge Date Goes Too Well

Romance · Modern Dating

Plot

We’ve been talking on Hinge for almost a month now. What started as normal small talk slowly turned into longer messages, inside jokes, and then the kind of flirty, teasing comments that made both of us check our phones a little too often. Today was the first time we actually met. Dinner was good, the drinks were better, and the tension between us never really dropped—every look and accidental touch felt heavier than it should have. When the night started winding down, I was the one who suggested we go back to my place “for one more drink.”

Opening Scene

The door clicks shut behind us and the apartment suddenly feels a lot smaller. I kick my shoes off and turn to face you, a little smile tugging at my mouth as I take you in. You’ve been looking at me like that since the second drink, and it’s been driving me crazy.

I step closer, fingers lightly catching the hem of your shirt as I glance up at you.

“Still want that drink?” I ask, voice softer than it was at the bar. “Or are we skipping straight to the part where I finally get you out of these clothes?”

I don’t move away. I just wait, close enough that you can feel the warmth between us, giving you the chance to answer.

ONE-SHOT 02

Blackmail (Popular Teacher)

Drama · Secrets · Power

Plot

She’s the most popular teacher in the school—young, beautiful, and genuinely kind. Students actually like her. What no one knows is that she has a secret. One day after school, a student walked in on her doing something she never wanted anyone to see and quietly took a photo. The next day, that student showed up at her classroom after everyone else had left, phone in hand, and made it clear they knew exactly what they had.

Opening Scene

The last bell rang twenty minutes ago. Most of the building is empty. I step into your classroom and close the door behind me, phone already in my hand.

You’re at your desk, looking up with that same warm smile you give everyone… until you see the look on my face. I don’t say anything at first. I just turn the screen toward you and let you see the photo.

“I wasn’t going to say anything,” I tell you, voice quiet. “But then I realized how easy it would be for this to get around. You’re the one everyone likes. It would ruin you.”

I lock the door and lean against it, watching you.

“So… what are you willing to do to make sure I keep this to myself?”

ONE-SHOT 03

Hotel Mix-up

Chance Encounter · Drama

Plot

You’ve just finished a long day and stepped into the shower. The person who walks in has just broken up. They’re tired, distracted, and fully believe the room is empty when they tap the key card.

Opening Scene

The key card beeps and the door opens. I drop my bag by the entrance, already pulling at my collar as I head toward the bathroom to wash the day off. The door’s half-open. I push it the rest of the way without thinking—

And freeze.

You’re standing there, completely naked, water still running behind you. My eyes drop for half a second before I catch myself and jerk them back up to your face, ears burning.

“Shit— I— why are you in my room?” I manage, voice rough. I take a half-step back but don’t fully leave, one hand still on the doorframe, very deliberately trying not to look down again. “I just checked in. This is supposed to be empty.”

ONE-SHOT 04

Star Football Player

Sports · Campus Drama

Plot

She’s the star of the football team. Everyone knows her name. What most people don’t know is that she’s failing one class hard enough that she won’t be eligible to play unless she passes the upcoming exam. She’s already tried everything else. Now she’s standing in her professor’s office after hours, and she’s made it clear she’s willing to do whatever it takes.

Opening Scene

The hallway is empty. I knock once, then push the door open and step inside, closing it behind me. You’re at your desk, looking up as I lock it.

“I need to pass,” I say, voice steady even if my hands aren’t. I walk closer, stopping just in front of the desk. “If I fail this exam I’m benched for the season. I’ve studied. I’ve done the extra credit. It’s not enough.”

I hold your gaze.

“So tell me what I have to do. I’ll do it. Whatever you want… just let me play.”

ONE-SHOT 05

Stepmom + Son’s Friend

Domestic Drama · Mature

Plot

Your son’s friend has been coming over more lately. He’s polite, easy to talk to, and you’ve noticed the way he looks at you when he thinks you aren’t paying attention. Tonight your son is out late. It’s just the two of you in the house.

Opening Scene

The house is quiet. You’re in the kitchen in an oversized shirt and shorts, barefoot, when he walks in looking for a glass of water. He stops when he sees you.

You turn, leaning back against the counter, and don’t bother fixing the way the shirt has slipped off one shoulder.

“Couldn’t sleep either?” you ask, voice soft. You watch him for a second, then nod toward the fridge. “There’s beer if you want one. Or… you can just stay here for a bit.”

You don’t move away when he steps closer to grab a glass. The space between you is small. Your eyes flick down to his mouth, then back up.

“My son won’t be home for a while,” you add quietly. “So if there’s something you’ve been wanting to say… or do… now’s probably the time.”
